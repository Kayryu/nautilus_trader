# AI Policy

AI tools may assist with this SDK, but the human contributor is responsible for every submitted
change and public communication. Review AI-assisted code in full, understand its behavior, and
verify claims with local tests before submission.

Do not submit third-party code without the necessary rights. AI tools cannot accept license or
contributor agreements or certify authorship for a person. Never put credentials or private keys
in prompts, source files, test fixtures, command arguments, logs, or published artifacts.

AI assistance does not require attribution. Do not list a tool or model as an author, co-author, or
contributor, or add a branded attribution footer. The contributor decides whether and how to
disclose assistance, subject to any applicable legal or contractual obligations.
